Radical transparency is how we work. Here is everything you need to know about our 7-day guarantee, 30/30/40 milestone pricing, intellectual property, and communication.

Sprints & 7-Day Guarantee

For qualifying web and rapid MVP builds, we scope a functional baseline documented in a signed MVP scope agreement. From kickoff, our engineering team works in a dedicated sprint. If we do not deliver your fully functional staging build within 7 business days, your project is 100% free.

Rapid websites, product landing pages, functional prototypes, and focused MVP tools. Larger enterprise SaaS platforms, multi-tenant databases, or complex ERPs are delivered through 2-to-4 week milestones, also structured on our 30/30/40 framework.

Milestone Payments & Pricing

We eliminate client risk by dividing payments across three objective checkpoints:

  • 30% Kickoff: Architecture, wireframes, and project initiation.
  • 30% Staging Build: Interactive, tested development build ready for your live hands-on inspection.
  • 40% Final Launch: Performance audit, security hardening, full source code handover, and production deploy.

You never pay the final balance until the milestone deliverables match the signed MVP scope document.

Before writing a line of code, we draft a concise MVP Scope Document listing exact features, user journeys, and acceptance criteria. You only approve and fund stages as verified deliverables are demonstrated on private staging.

We accept international bank wire transfers, Stripe (credit/debit cards), Razorpay, and direct bank transfers (INR for domestic Indian clients; USD, EUR, GBP for global clients). Invoices include formal GST or corporate tax breakdown.

Ownership & Intellectual Property

Engineering & Support

Every delivered project includes a complimentary 30-Day Bug-Fix Warranty. If any reproducible issue emerges within 30 days of launch, our engineers fix it immediately at zero additional cost. We also provide ongoing maintenance retainers if desired.

You communicate directly with studio leadership and senior engineers via dedicated Slack or WhatsApp channels, backed by asynchronous video walkthroughs and email milestone summaries. No middleman account managers.
