1. Milestone-Protected Engagements

Unlike traditional agencies that demand 100% upfront payment, ZENSTRO breaks client engagements into structured milestones tied directly to verifiable deliverables:

  • Stage 1 (30% Commencement): Project setup, technical architecture, schema modeling, and discovery.
  • Stage 2 (30% Development): Interactive staging environment, backend APIs, and core features.
  • Stage 3 (40% Completion): Final QA, performance optimization, security checks, code handover, and production launch.

Core Principle: You only pay for progress that has been delivered, reviewed, and approved according to the signed MVP Scope Document.

2. Cancellation by Project Stage

If you choose to cancel a project before completion, refunds are calculated objectively based on active milestone status:

Timing of Cancellation Refund Eligibility Deliverables Provided
Before work commences (Within 48h of deposit, zero engineering started) 100% Refund of the commencement deposit (minus third-party payment processing fees). None.
During Stage 1 (Commencement / Architecture) Commencement deposit is earned to cover allocated engineering and architecture hours. Stages 2 & 3 are cancelled with zero further liability. All architecture notes, UI wireframes, and database diagrams created to date.
During Stage 2 (Active Development) Stage 1 deposit is non-refundable. Stage 2 deposit is pro-rated based on verified completed components. Stage 3 remains unbilled. Current Git repository commit history and staging build assets.
Upon Stage 3 Final Approval Non-refundable once final code is accepted and delivered to production. Covered by our 30-Day Bug-Fix Warranty. Full source code, production deployment, and asset ownership.

3. Milestone Acceptance & Review Period

To ensure fair evaluation, every deliverable is subject to a formal review process:

  • Review Window: You have 5 business days from delivery of a milestone staging build to test functionality against the signed MVP Scope Document.
  • Revision Requests: If any deliverable deviates from agreed specifications, we will revise and rectify it within the milestone scope at no additional charge.
  • Formal Sign-Off: Approval of a milestone (or commencement of the subsequent development stage) constitutes formal acceptance of that milestone's deliverables.

4. Rapid 7-Day Sprint Guarantee Conditions

For qualifying standard projects designated under our Rapid 7-Day MVP Delivery Sprint:

  • If ZENSTRO fails to deliver the agreed MVP scope on staging within 7 business days solely due to internal engineering delay (excluding client response delays, delayed asset provision, or mid-sprint scope modifications), the sprint fee will be waived or refunded in accordance with the specific sprint agreement.
  • Client-side delays (such as taking longer than 24 hours to supply required credentials or approve design checkpoints) pause the 7-day guarantee clock accordingly.

5. How to Request a Cancellation or Refund

To submit a cancellation or refund request, follow this simple procedure:

  1. Send a formal written notice from the email address listed in your MVP Scope Document via our Contact Page or direct project thread.
  2. Include your project name, date of engagement, and detailed reason for the request.
  3. Our team will acknowledge receipt within 24 business hours and review the project repository status.

6. Refund Processing Timelines

Approved refunds are processed promptly:

  • Refund decisions are communicated in writing within 3 business days of receipt.
  • Approved funds are issued within 5 to 10 business days directly back to the original method of payment (bank transfer, Stripe, or payment gateway).
  • A formal invoice credit note is generated for your company's accounting records.

7. Amicable Dispute Redressal

If you have any questions or feel a milestone evaluation was not handled fairly, please reach out directly to our Founder via the Contact Form marked "Client Redressal". We are committed to prompt, reasonable, and amicable resolution.
